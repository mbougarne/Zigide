# Research: Correcting Immutable Records

## Epistemic Boundary

This is an educational explanation of a repository-record correction, not a claim about private model internals.

## Append-Only Correction

An immutable ledger does not erase mistakes. It preserves the original artifact and appends a correction that identifies the discrepancy and the corrected interpretation. This keeps the publication history auditable while preventing the mistaken value from becoming the policy used by future records.

## What This Record Can and Cannot Teach

It can teach why write-once records require explicit correction entries. It cannot retroactively change the original transcription or make a historical record appear as if it had been written differently.
