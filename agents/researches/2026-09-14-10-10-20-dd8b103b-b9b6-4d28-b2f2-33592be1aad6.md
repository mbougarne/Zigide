# Research: Turning Roadmap Outcomes into Exit Evidence

## Evidence Map

An exit checklist is a traceability map, not a second status board. It connects each roadmap outcome to the ticket that must produce the final evidence and to the command or artifact that can verify it. Prerequisite tickets can contribute evidence, but one final owner keeps completion decisions unambiguous.

## Evidence Categories

- Automated evidence is a repeatable command whose pass/fail result is meaningful, such as `zig build test`.
- Benchmark evidence is a report that includes workload, hardware, build mode, and measurement context.
- Manual evidence records observations that automation cannot establish, such as IME and VoiceOver behavior.
- Decision evidence is an accepted ADR or a superseding ADR backed by the spike results.

## Repository Application

The Milestone 0 checklist now maps the three roadmap exit conditions and six deliverables to the final tickets ZIT-006, ZIT-007, ZIT-019, ZIT-026, ZIT-033, and ZIT-041. The already-existing pinned-toolchain policy is explicitly recorded as baseline evidence under ZIT-001, so it remains traceable without inventing a new implementation ticket.

This record describes observable repository structure and verification practice. It does not claim that the future Milestone 0 tickets or their product evidence are complete.
