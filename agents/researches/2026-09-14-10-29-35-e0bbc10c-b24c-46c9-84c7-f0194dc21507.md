# Research: Evidence Reports Must Separate Baseline Health from Exit Readiness

## Evidence Levels

A repository can have healthy tooling while the product milestone remains incomplete. A passing build of the checker, a passing checker unit test, and passing Markdown/trace validation establish baseline repository health. They do not establish that product modules, services, protocol behavior, benchmarks, or UI behavior exist.

## Reporting Rule

An evidence report should classify each item as verified, partial, blocked, or not evidenced, link the responsible ticket, and state the exact missing proof. This prevents a passing infrastructure command from being misread as proof of a future product behavior.

## Application to Zigide

The ZIT-002 report records Zig `0.16.0`, macOS arm64, local command results, accepted ADR-0001 through ADR-0003, and the still-Proposed ADR-0004. It also records the missing product and spike evidence and keeps ZIT-002 In Progress until its dependencies produce that evidence.

This is an educational explanation of observable repository evidence, not a claim about hidden model internals or external CI state.
